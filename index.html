Learn any piece from a MIDI file

Open a .mid file (most sheet-music sites offer one next to the sheet). You'll get sheet music and falling notes side by side, the chords, practice sections, notes and progress tracking.

  • Tap or hover the falling notes to see which notes are played together.
  • Set a hand to Mute to play that part yourself while the app plays the other.
  • Drag on the timeline to loop a passage; slow it down with Speed.
  • Double-tap with two fingers anywhere to jump back to the start of the loop.
